# Downstream harvest before v0.1.9, 2026-10-15

## Decision

- A newly adopted downstream reviewed its own development practice as part of its
  adoption and sent seven feedback Issues (#37-#43): three settled practices that
  passed the generality gate and four template defects found while adopting. All
  seven landed for v0.1.9.
- A second adopter sent one feedback Issue (#44); it landed as a shared knowledge
  record and a shared memory record.
- The incremental check of the other adopters found one adopter with new
  development-plane commits since the v0.1.8 watermark; all were project-local or
  already covered by `llm-credentials`. The rest had not moved.

Adopter names, paths and private decisions stayed in the ignored local state.

## Why

The release checklist requires a harvest before each release. When an adopter
reviews its own practice through `template-feedback`, its Issues are the harvest
result for that adopter; the maintainer still checks the others from their
watermarks.

## Apply

Start the next run from the recorded watermarks. Adopters pinned at v0.1.6 or
v0.1.8 take v0.1.9 through `template-upgrade`, moving local edits into each
shared skill's `## In this repository` section (#39).
