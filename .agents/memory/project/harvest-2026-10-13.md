# First downstream harvest, 2026-10-13

## Decision

The first `template-harvest` run found four distinct adopters reachable on the
Linux device (three settled, one mid-adoption) plus one path that could not be
resolved, and classified 13 deltas across the three settled adopters.

- Two candidates became shared content: the `issue-workflow` skill and the two
  `memory/shared/` records for the per-Issue worktree and its close-out.
- Three further shared candidates were landed directly on `dev`: asset-link
  inheritance in `setup-worktree.sh`, the `--also`/`--skip` stop-and-verify
  behaviour in `migrate-assets.sh`, and a template-relationship section in
  `DEV.md`.
- One delta was already upstream as a rule, the rest were project-local or drift.
- One skill extension point (status of live runs during migration) and the
  mid-adoption adopter are watch items, not sources yet.

Every landed change shipped in v0.1.7. Adopter names, paths and private decisions
stayed in the ignored local state.

## Why

Two adopters had settled a full Issue/worktree/PR procedure for Herdr-managed
development and cross-ported it by hand, while this repository documented the
lifecycle only as prose; its `memory/shared/` was empty. The run also found a
data-loss edge in `migrate-assets.sh` (an unknown `.project/` entry was dropped
silently) and an asset-link gap in `setup-worktree.sh` (a fresh worktree inherited
no mapping from the primary checkout), both of which every adopter with external
assets inherits.

## Apply

Re-run the harvest before the next release, per the `DEV.md` release checklist.
Start from each adopter's recorded watermark; the mid-adoption adopter is not a
source until its registry reaches its integration branch. Keep the adopter set and
per-adopter landing in the ignored `.project/state/harvest/adopters.yaml`, and keep
names, paths and private decisions out of tracked files and Issues. A candidate
becomes shared content only when it passes the skill's generality gate.
